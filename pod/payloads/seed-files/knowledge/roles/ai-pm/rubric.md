# AI Product Engineer — Rubric

## What we're hiring for
Someone who can both ship AI features and own product judgment on them. Not a "data scientist", not a "PM who talks to engineers". A builder who thinks about what to make next.

## Must-haves
- **Shipped an AI feature into production users could actually touch.** Not a notebook, not a demo, not "we explored". Live for ≥ 4 weeks.
- **Has shipped something independently end-to-end.** Side project, OSS, internal tool — anything where they owned scope + execution.
- **Writes well.** Sample writing required (blog, README, design doc). Founder-team communication is mostly writing.
- **Comfortable with ambiguity.** Has worked in 0→1 or in a domain with unclear ground truth.

## Nice-to-haves
- Built with an agentic framework (LangChain, AutoGen, Lemma, etc.)
- Has hired or managed even one person
- Strong evals/measurement instinct (knows what "good" looks like for AI output)
- Indian context — has shipped to non-English-first users

## Red flags
- "I'd love to learn AI" — we need someone who already builds
- Career arc that suggests they need a big team / clear PM
- Heavy CV but no shipping receipts (links to live things)

## Calibration anchors
- *Strong hire*: shipped agentic feature owned end-to-end, can defend product decisions on it, writes precisely
- *Hire*: shipped something real, growth trajectory clear, judgment is forming
- *No hire*: smart but talks abstractly about AI; no production scars

## What we'll test
- 25-min intro: motivation + one shipping war story
- 60-min product round: pick one of our open product calls, talk through how you'd decide
- 60-min build round: pair on a real Hiremind/agent design question
- 45-min founder chat: judgment + voice + drive
