# AI Product Engineer — Pitch

## The one-sentence pitch
*"Come build the agentic layer that decides what our users actually need, then ship it before anyone asks for it."*

## Why this role is unusual
- **Founding-team scope.** Direct ownership of product surface, not a sub-component.
- **Real users from day one.** Not an internal tool, not a dashboard, not "AI for our existing process". You're shipping to the people paying us.
- **Subscription-aligned tooling.** Bring your own Claude/Codex/Cursor — we cover infra not your IDE.
- **Hiring loop you'll join.** You'll have a peer hired in the same way you were.

## What you'll trade off
- We move weekly, not quarterly. If you need long planning cycles, this is wrong.
- You will write prose. A lot. We document decisions before we code them.
- We don't have a manager class. You'll partner with the founder directly.

## Comp band
- Cash + meaningful equity. Numbers in the offer conversation, not the cold note.

## What good week one looks like
- Read the codebase end-to-end, identify the one feature you'd ship first
- Ship a small but visible fix to production
- Run a working session with the founder on what you'd build next
