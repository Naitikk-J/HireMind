# Founding Design Engineer — Rubric

## What we're hiring for
The rare person who designs AND ships their own designs. Owns the visual + interaction surface of the product end-to-end.

## Must-haves
- **A live product where the design and the code are both theirs.** No "I designed it, someone else built it."
- **Strong opinions on motion, typography, density.** We can tell from their work, not their words.
- **Cares about the unfashionable parts** — empty states, error states, loading states.

## Nice-to-haves
- Editorial / publication design background
- Has worked on internal tools (where density actually matters)
- Open-source UI work that other people use

## Red flags
- Beautiful Dribbble shots, no shipping
- Strong opinions on tools, weak opinions on outcomes

## What we'll test
- Portfolio walk-through (45 min)
- Live redesign of an existing Hiremind screen (60 min)
- Founder chat (30 min)
