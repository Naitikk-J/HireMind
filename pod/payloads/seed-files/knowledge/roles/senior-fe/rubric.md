# Senior Frontend Engineer — Rubric

## What we're hiring for
A frontend engineer who ships polished consumer-grade interfaces fast, has taste, and treats performance + accessibility as defaults not chores.

## Must-haves
- **5+ years building production React** (or equivalent — Vue/Svelte fine).
- **A live URL we can open** showing something polished they shipped.
- **Comfortable owning design from a rough spec** — pixel decisions don't need a designer for every screen.
- **TypeScript fluency.** Not "I'm learning it." Production for ≥ 2 years.

## Nice-to-haves
- Built design systems / component libraries
- Animation chops (Framer Motion or hand-rolled)
- Owned web performance metrics on a real product
- Has shipped on Lemma, Vercel, or similar agentic/serverless deployment

## Red flags
- Portfolio dominated by templates and clones, no original product work
- Strong tooling opinions but no shipping history with them
- "Pixel perfect" without the taste to back it up

## Calibration anchors
- *Strong hire*: live product, evident taste, ships fast, can architect a system not just a page
- *Hire*: solid output, judgment forming, will reach senior within a year
- *No hire*: skilled but slow / template-driven / no opinion

## What we'll test
- 25-min intro: walk us through their best shipped work
- 60-min build round: live-build a small UI from a Figma reference
- 60-min systems round: design our next major surface end-to-end
- 45-min founder chat
