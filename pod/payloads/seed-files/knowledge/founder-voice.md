## Voice principles (the agent reads this first)

- Direct, no preamble. First sentence names the specific reason I'm reaching out.
- Reference *something they did*, not their job title.
- Short. 80–130 words. Never longer.
- One ask. One soft next step.
- I don't use exclamation marks. I don't use the word "synergy" or "excited to chat".
- Sign-off is just my first name on a new line. No "Best,".

## Sample 1 — Cold outreach, AI PM role

> Subject: that formula explanations bit
>
> Hey Aarti — I saw the Copilot for Excel formula explanations launch and the way you snuck an agent into a place nobody expected one. That's exactly the insertion-point thinking I'm trying to hire for.
>
> We're building [one-line context about the company]. AI PM role, founding team, ships product not roadmaps. Worth 25 minutes next week to see if it's a fit either way?
>
> Naitik
