How it works

Four steps. No spreadsheets. No ATS.

Hiremind sits behind a Telegram bot and a Gmail inbox. The dashboard is for review, not for data entry — every row already has signal by the time you see it.

1 Forward

Drop a profile into Telegram

LinkedIn URL, voice note from a coffee, or a referral message. The bot ingests anything.

2 Triage

AI scores + writes a dossier

Real strengths, real concerns, role-fit rubric. Founder-friendly. No emoji theatre.

3 Approve

One-tap outreach in your voice

Cold intro, screening request, offer, or a kind AI-drafted rejection. You always sign off.

4 Meet

Calendar + Meet auto-scheduled

3 free slots pulled from your calendar. Candidate picks one, Meet link in their inbox.

What's inside

The 30 minutes a founder spends on hiring — compressed into 3.

Built around the actual rituals of founder-led hiring: 1-on-1 reviews, drafts queue, side-by-side compare, AI screen. Everything operator-grade.

AI-written dossiers

One paragraph summary. Why interesting, Concerns, Talking points — always the same three sections, never invented.

Founder-voice drafts

Trained on your past replies. Cold intro, screen request, offer, rejection. You review, edit, tap send. Never sounds like a recruiter.

Side-by-side compare

Pick 2–4 candidates. Read down a column for one person, or across a row to evaluate one criterion across the slate.

Free-busy aware scheduling

Three real slots from your calendar in every intro. Candidate picks, Meet link auto-generated, invite in their inbox.

AI async interview

Generated questions for the role's must-haves. Candidate opens their public status page, runs the screen, pastes a transcript code back.

Kind AI rejections

Cites the actual gap from the dossier, acknowledges what you did like. Specific enough that candidates leave the loop with dignity, not silence.
